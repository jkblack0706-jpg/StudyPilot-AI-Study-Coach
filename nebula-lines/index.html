How to play

  1. Click an open square to place your magenta beacon.
  2. The cyan AI responds after your turn.
  3. Be the first to connect four consecutive beacons horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.
  4. Asteroids are permanently blocked. The board filling without a winner is a draw.

About the AI

The opponent uses a JavaScript minimax search with alpha-beta pruning, tactical win/block detection, and positional evaluation. It responds automatically—no API key required.
